The game: Pass the Gita

People keep appearing. Put a Bhagavad Gita in every pair of hands before the clock runs out.

  • Tap a person and a book pops into their hands. Some are busy with a phone, so say hello first.

  • Beat the goal before time ends. Every 100 books earns 10 bonus books, and streaks of 5 earn one more.

  • Restock between levels. Donors match every book you gave, one for one, and the levels get faster.

Before you start

Get ready to give

  • Tap every person who pops up, anywhere on the screen. A book appears in their hands.

  • Be quick. People walk away if you wait, and that breaks your streak. Every 100 books earns 10 bonus books.
